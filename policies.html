Privacy

One policy per product.

Our products collect genuinely different things — one needs no account at all, another stores a name you choose so friends can see your times, and another is a journal you sign in to. A single policy covering all of them would have to be vague enough to be useless, or wrong about at least one. So there is one per product.

Choose your product

  • 13 games · Android Card Shark No account, no sign-up. Your chips, stats and settings stay on your device. Google AdMob may use an advertising ID to serve ads, Play Billing handles the optional Pro purchase, and Firebase Analytics records anonymous usage. We hold nothing about you on our own servers. Read the Card Shark policy
  • Puzzle · Android Thunk No account — but one opt-in feature stores data. Progress lives on your device. If you choose to play alongside friends, a display name you pick and your level times are saved to our Firebase database so they can see them. Nothing is written until you add a friend. AdMob and Play Billing as above. Read the Thunk policy
  • Web & Android Choptick An account is required, and your trades are the product. Email address and a hashed password, the trades and reflections you log, connection tokens and the raw fills your broker tools send, and plan status from the payment processor. No ads and no tracking SDKs. Read the Choptick policy on choptick.app
  • 🚀 Free · runs in your browser Rocket Scooter Race Nothing leaves your browser — there is no policy to read, because there is nothing to disclose. The page makes no network requests once it has loaded. The racer names you type and your settings are kept in your browser's local storage so the page remembers them next visit; clearing your browser data removes them. No account, no ads, no analytics, and nothing is ever sent to us. Play it now

Common to all of them

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with anyone beyond the service providers named in each policy.

Deleting it. For the games, uninstalling removes what is on your device; Thunk's friends record is deleted on request by email. Choptick has a self-serve route at choptick.app/delete-account.

Anything unclear? Email support@innovizea.com and say which product you're asking about.
