About these games

The Card Shark games are simulations built for learning: you play with virtual chips, and the trainers grade you against the same engine the table itself plays by.

Where a side bet has a published house edge, we show it — including the ugly ones. A bonus bet is more fun when you know what it costs.

There is no real-money wagering and no cash prizes in any of our apps. Practising on a simulator does not change the house edge on a real casino floor.
