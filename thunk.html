What makes it different

  • The rules arrive one at a time

    Twenty of them, each introduced on its own level and then combined with the ones you already know. Some constrain the board, some constrain the tray — a shape you may not use, a piece you have to place first, an order you have to follow.

  • No lives, no energy, no timers between attempts

    Stars measure how fast you solved it, so the intended thing to do after a slow finish is play it again and beat yourself. Anything that meters retries would punish exactly the behaviour the game is built around.

  • One daily board, shared by everyone

    Seeded from the UTC date, so it is the same puzzle worldwide on the same day. Hints and skips are switched off on it — a shared time only means something if everybody played it the same way.

  • A share that doesn't spoil it

    The share card draws the outline you were given, never the solution you found. Posting your result cannot ruin the board for the person reading it.

  • It never runs out

    Levels are generated on demand from a seed and verified solvable before you see one, rather than drawn from a fixed pack. There is no last level to reach.

  • Play alongside friends

    Optionally connect with people you invite and see their times next to yours. It is opt-in — nothing is published until you add a friend.
